## Where we stand

:::kpi[Requests answered within a day]{value="94%" change="+6 pp" since="Q2"}
:::

:::kpi[Median waiting time]{value="3.2 h" change="-1.1 h" since="Q2" good=down}
:::

:::notes
Both figures improved for the third quarter in a row. The new triage rules did most of it.
:::

## What we learned

:::statement
A third of chat requests arrive after five, and wait until morning.
:::

:::notes
This is the one finding to remember. Customers who write in the evening wait the longest.
:::

## What we change

1. **Evening chat** from November, until 21:00 on working days.
2. **Evening figures** reported on their own.
3. **A review** at the end of the fourth quarter.

:::notes
The shift plan is agreed with the team. We will report back in January.
:::
