## Where requests come from

:::chart{type=hbar caption="Requests by channel, third quarter 2026" unit=""}
| Channel | Requests |
|---|---|
| E-mail to the support address | 1840 |
| Chat on the website | 1260 |
| Phone during office hours | 710 |
| Contact form | 390 |
:::

Chat requests grew by a third since the second quarter, most of the growth in the evening, when nobody answers chat today.

::::decision[Staff chat in the evening]{status=accepted date=2026-10-02 by="Head of support"}
From November, two people answer chat until 21:00 on working days.
:::context
A third of chat requests arrive after 17:00 and wait until the next morning.
:::
:::options
- **Evening chat**: answers the same day; two shifts to plan.
- **An automatic reply**: no extra shifts; customers still wait until morning.
:::
:::consequences
- The shift plan changes from November.
- We measure evening waiting times separately in the fourth quarter.
:::
::::
