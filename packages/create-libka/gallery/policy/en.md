This policy takes effect on 1 November 2026 and replaces the chat retention note of March 2026.

## Why we keep records

We keep a record of each conversation so a colleague can continue it, so we can answer a complaint, and so we can learn which questions customers ask most.

## Who it applies to

Everyone in the support team, and anyone who reads support conversations for another team.

## The rules

1. Keep a conversation only in the support tools, never in personal notes or e-mail.
2. Keep it no longer than the period in the table below.
3. Delete payment card numbers from a conversation as soon as you see them.
4. Share a conversation outside the support team only with the customer's consent or when the law requires it.

## How long we keep records

:::table{caption="Retention periods for customer conversations"}
| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Chat and e-mail conversations | 24 months | deleted automatically |
| Phone call notes | 24 months | deleted automatically |
| Conversations about a complaint | 36 months after it is closed | deleted by the team lead |
:::

:::callout[A customer may ask us to delete their records sooner]{variant=info}
Forward the request to the team lead the same day; we answer within one month.
:::
