Each record says what we decided, when and by whom. A record is never rewritten: a later decision replaces it.

:::::decision[DR 3: One tool for chat and e-mail]{status=accepted date=2026-09-15 by="Head of support"}
From January 2027 we answer chat and e-mail in one shared help desk.
::::context
Today chat and e-mail live in two tools, so a customer who writes in both is two cases, and nobody sees the whole conversation.
::::
::::options
:::comparison[The tools we compared]{highlight="A shared help desk"}
| Criterion | Keep two tools | A shared help desk | Build our own |
|---|---|---|---|
| One conversation per customer | no | yes | yes |
| Ready by January | yes | yes | no |
| Cost a year | EUR 9,000 | EUR 11,000 | EUR 40,000 |
:::
::::
::::consequences
- Every agent is trained on the new tool in December.
- Chat and e-mail records move to the new tool; the policy on keeping records applies to them unchanged.
::::
:::::

:::::decision[DR 4: Answer every e-mail within one working day]{status=proposed}
We propose to answer every e-mail within one working day, as we do chat; the decision is due at the November team meeting.
::::context
Half of the e-mails are answered within a day today, and customers who wait longer write again.
