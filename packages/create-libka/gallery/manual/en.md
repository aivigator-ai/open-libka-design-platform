This manual is for everyone who answers chat on the website. New team members read it before their first shift.

## Before you start

:::callout[Log in to the chat tool before your shift]{variant=info}
The chat queue opens at 8:00. Log in at 7:50, so the first customers do not wait.
:::

You need the chat tool, the customer records and the answers library open in your browser.

## Answer a chat request

:::steps
1. **Greet the customer by name** The chat tool shows the name the customer gave.
2. **Read the whole message first** Many customers ask two things in one message.
3. **Check the customer's record** Open earlier requests before you answer.
4. **Answer, or say when you will** If you need more than five minutes, say so and when you will be back.
5. **Close the chat** Ask whether there is anything else, then close the request with its topic.
:::

## How we write

::::compare
:::do[Say what happens next]
"I have sent the new invoice to your e-mail; it arrives within ten minutes."
:::
:::dont[Leave the customer guessing]
"Done."
:::
::::

Write in short sentences. Use the customer's words for their problem, not our internal names.

## When to hand over

:::callout[Hand over a complaint about money]{variant=warning}
A request for a refund over EUR 200 goes to the team lead. Tell the customer who will answer and by when.
:::

Hand over a chat in the chat tool, with one sentence on what the customer needs, so they never repeat it.

## Words we use

:::glossary
- **Queue** The chats waiting for an answer, oldest first.
- **Hand-over** Passing a chat to a colleague or the team lead, with a note.
- **Answers library** The approved answers to common questions.
:::

## At the end of your shift

:::checklist
- [ ] Hand over every open chat
- [ ] Note anything the next shift should know
- [ ] Log out of the chat tool
:::
